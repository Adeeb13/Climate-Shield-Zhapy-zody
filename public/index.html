Assumptions & limits

  • Per-region terrain and soil values are illustrative, not measured.
  • Weights are hand-set; nothing is trained or validated.
  • Linear scaling of rainfall ignores drainage, tides and rivers.
  • Largest differences point to where closer attention may be warranted.

Mission · Lives and assets protected

ERA5 and GFS have a server-side ingestion script (written untested against live services) whose JSON output this page can load. The other three are planned. Nothing is connected live here.

How “protected” is measured

Lead time gained, hit rate, false-alarm rate, missed-event rate, and exposure covered by pre-positioned resources. The last one is modeled in Response. No real count of lives or assets saved is claimed; that needs back-testing with agencies.

Where we aim to go further

Platforms such as Sustera, Jupiter Intelligence, One Concern, Descartes Labs and 7Analytics are reference points, with no affiliation. Our aim is one open, explainable loop with uncertainty on every output, from hazard to cascade to response to alert. Superiority is claimed only after benchmarking.

Response · Allocate resources under uncertainty

Each of 200 Monte Carlo runs perturbs the rainfall scenario. A greedy optimizer then places each response unit (pump, boat, team) where it removes the most expected exposure, and the result is compared with an even split.

RL agent · REINFORCE policy gradient

A softmax policy learns from scratch where to send each unit. It sees forecast exposure and diminishing returns, and is rewarded by the exposure mitigated in randomly sampled rainfall outcomes. Training runs in your browser.
